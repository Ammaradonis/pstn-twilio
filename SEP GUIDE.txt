The Vapi agent is set up and verified. It can't place real prospect calls yet, because it's missing three things only you can provide: your business name, your name, and Google Calendar access.

How it works
- Starting a call: on the Dial page, paste a number, pick the target state (Alabama is the default and your choice is remembered), and press Call with AI agent. It calls from +1 (667) 220-6726.
- The conversation:
  - The agent says it's an AI in its first sentence, then asks for the owner.
  - With the owner, it follows your playbook: who answers during evening classes, why missed calls cost enrollments, then the offer of a 20-minute Google Meet.
  - It never invents statistics and never quotes a price.
  - A front desk person gets asked for the owner's name and the best time to reach them.
  - "Don't call again" gets acknowledged out loud and the number is blocked for future AI calls.
- Timezones: the state sets the prospect's timezone. States that span two zones show a timezone picker. The server refuses calls outside 8 AM–9 PM prospect time.
- Booking: the agent never works out times itself. Your API reads your Google Calendar and offers open 20-minute slots between 9 AM and 6 PM on weekdays, in the prospect's local time. Slots are at least 2 hours out, with 10 minutes of padding around your events. It reads the email back and waits for a "yes", then creates the event with a Meet link and sends the invite.
- Results: each call appears live under the Dial page with its outcome (booked, callback, gatekeeper, voicemail, and so on), the booked time with the Meet link, a summary and the recording.
- Callbacks: schools calling 667 back reach the same agent, with the state and timezone of the original call. Until your names are set, callbacks get a polite "we can't take your call right now".

I used your API rather than Vapi's built-in Google Calendar tools, because Vapi's tools can't create Meet links.
