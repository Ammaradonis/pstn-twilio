READ ME FIRST

12 sequences, 6 emails each, 72 emails total. One file per call status.

VARIABLES
Every sequence uses all 12 of these somewhere across its 6 emails. If your sending tool uses different names, find and replace them.

{{school_name}}    school name
{{website}}        school website URL
{{rating}}         Google rating (example 4.9)
{{review_count}}   number of Google reviews
{{category}}       Google category (example Martial arts school)
{{street}}         street name
{{city}}           city
{{state}}          state
{{called_number}}  the school number you called
{{my_number}}      the number you called from, so they can find it in their call log
{{call_time}}      time of your call (example 6:42pm on Tuesday)
{{call_note}}      your personal note on the call

NOTES
{{call_note}} sits on its own line in email 1 of every sequence. Write it as a full first person sentence so it reads naturally. Example: A kid picked up, said hold on, and I waited almost 2 minutes before it cut off.

matboss.online only appears in emails 5 and 6. Emails 1 to 4 never mention the site.

Every email pushes the demo line (667) 220-6726 and asks for a reply with two things in this order. First how the demo call felt. Then one thing about their school that exposes a blind spot.

The typos, missing apostrophes and plain punctuation are on purpose. Dont let anyone clean them up.

No hyphenated words anywhere in the emails. The only hyphen is inside the demo phone number.
