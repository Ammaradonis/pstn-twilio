**School websites and Google/Yelp/business listings are the most direct places** you’d expect a martial arts school owner’s (or head instructor’s) contact email. Many list an `info@`, `sensei@`, or personal Gmail/Yahoo address on the Contact page, About page, or in the footer. Owners often claim/update these listings themselves.

Beyond individual school sites, here are the main associations, organizations, directories, competitions, and communities that commonly surface emails for school owners/instructors:

### National & Multi-Style Federations / Associations with School Directories
These often publish member school or club lists that include names, websites, phones, and emails:

- **United States Martial Arts Federation (USMAF)** — National school/club directory with many explicit emails for owners/instructors (e.g., by state).
- **United States of America Wushu Kungfu Federation (USAWKF)** — School search with emails and websites for affiliated schools.
- **Martial Arts Teachers’ Association (MATA)** — Runs BestMartialArtsSchools.com and MartialArtsTeachers.com directories aimed at school owners and instructors; free listings for visibility and networking.
- **USAdojo / Martial Arts Schools Directory** (Martial Arts Enterprises) — Large classified-style directory of schools, businesses, and organizations; supports contact via listings.
- **British Martial Arts & Boxing Association (BMABA)** — UK National Register of clubs and instructors (verification-focused).
- Style-specific or regional ones (examples): Australian Martial Arts Industry Association members, various national karate/taekwondo/judo federations, World Karate Confederation member orgs, World Kuo Shu Federation members, International Mixed Martial Arts Federation national bodies, etc. Many list affiliated schools or contacts.

### Dedicated Martial Arts Directories
These aggregate thousands of schools worldwide and frequently include or link to contact details:

- The Martial Arts Directory (themartialartsdirectory.com) — Claims 63,000+ schools.
- Dojos.com, Dojos.info, DojoMap (DojoDesk), Dojo Atlas, martialartsdirectory.org.
- World Martial Arts Media Directory.
- Commercial data sources (e.g., DataLane-style “martial arts school owner email lists”) compile owner-level contacts from public business registrations, Google, membership software, etc. (paid).

### Competitions, Tournaments & Sanctioning Bodies
Tournament sites, registration platforms, and sanctioning orgs often list participating schools, head coaches, or club contacts (sometimes with emails for team registration or results):

- Platforms like Kihapp (tournament management software) surface event organizers and registered teams/schools.
- Local/regional circuits (e.g., NY Tournament Circuit, Invictus Games Open Martial Arts Tournaments) and larger events publish school packets or contact lists.
- Sanctioning bodies (ISCF, various karate/MMA federations) maintain gym/school affiliations.
- Event pages and post-tournament results frequently name schools and coaches.

### Bigger Communities & Networking Spaces
- **MATA** and similar professional associations for school owners (curriculum, business training, networking).
- Style-specific international federations (karate, BJJ, taekwondo, kung fu/wushu, judo, etc.) and their national branches — many maintain member school lists or “find a school” tools.
- Facebook groups, Instagram, LinkedIn pages for martial arts school owners/instructors, and local dojo networks.
- Forums, Reddit communities (style-specific or r/martialarts), and media sites (World Martial Arts Media, USAdojo articles/events).

### Practical Tips on Where Emails Appear
- **Highest yield**: Individual school websites + Google Business Profile / Yelp.
- **Organized lists**: Federation member directories (USMAF, USAWKF, national governing bodies) and MATA-style directories.
- **Indirect but useful**: Tournament registration/contact pages, association “find a school” tools, and claimed directory listings.
- Many smaller or independent schools use personal emails; larger ones or franchises prefer generic `info@` or contact forms. Listings go out of date, so cross-check the school’s own site.

Start with a specific style or location + “school directory” or the federation name for that art, then dig into the school’s own contact page for the most current owner/instructor email.
